How GovScheme NLP Works

A transparent overview of our 7-stage Natural Language Processing pipeline.

Step 1
Natural Language Input

The user provides their personal details, family income, location, occupation, and scheme requirements in unstructured English prose.

Step 2
NLP Preprocessing

Text cleaning, lowercasing, sentence segmentation, tokenization, stop-word removal, stemming, and WordNet lemmatization.

Step 3
Attribute Extraction

Regular expressions, entity dictionaries, and optional spaCy NER extract specific fields (Age, Income in INR, State, Occupation, Category).

Step 4
Semantic Normalization

Synonym mapping converts diverse expressions (e.g. "cultivator" ➔ farmer, "undergraduate" ➔ student, "studies" ➔ Education) to standard canonical entities.

Step 5
TF-IDF Scheme Search

scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer calculates cosine similarity between user query tokens and scheme corpus documents to rank text relevance.

Step 6
Eligibility Matching Engine

Rule-based evaluator compares extracted attributes with structured scheme criteria to generate MATCHED, NOT_MATCHED, or NOT_PROVIDED flags.

Step 7
Explainable Results & Official Links

The system displays schemes ordered by Profile Match Score, accompanied by an explicit breakdown of matched criteria and official portal links for verification.
